2. Lightweight models (src/deskflow/ml/)
Everything uses fixed random_state = 42 and sklearn Pipelines fitted on training data only.
- Regression on target_total_ratio. Baselines: ConstantMedian, Persistence
  (probe_total_ratio at t), RollingMean (probe_total_ratio_mean_12s). Models: Ridge,
  RandomForestRegressor (at most 100 trees, max_depth at most 10), XGBRegressor (at most
  150 trees, max_depth at most 5).
- Classification on issue_now (healthy, cpu_pressure, memory_pressure, disk_pressure,
  background_load; exclude combo and transition rows). Baseline: DummyClassifier. Models:
  LogisticRegression (balanced), RandomForestClassifier (balanced, small), XGBClassifier.
- Anomaly: IsolationForest only (100 trees, max_samples 256) on healthy training rows; the
  threshold is the 99th percentile of training healthy scores.
- Tuning: tiny fixed grids (at most 8 combinations per model) with GroupKFold on run_id
  (at most 4 folds). Test split is touched once, for final evaluation. No randomized search.
- Refuse to train with clear errors if train rows < 200 or any class has < 30 train rows.
  Warn prominently when test rows < 200, test runs < 4, or only one machine_id exists.

3. Metrics (unit tested)
- Regression: MAE, RMSE, R2, tolerance accuracy (share within 0.25 of true ratio), and
  slow-detection precision/recall/F1 at SLOW_THRESHOLD 1.5.
- Classification: accuracy, balanced accuracy, macro F1, per-class P/R/F1, confusion matrix.
- Anomaly: ROC-AUC, recall at threshold, false alarm rate on healthy test rows, and what it
  says about combo rows.
- Breakdown of MAE and F1 by scenario, intensity and pattern.
- Block bootstrap over run_id (300 resamples, fixed seed) for 95 percent intervals on MAE,
  R2 and macro F1, and on each model's improvement over Persistence. The report may say a
  model is "better than" a baseline only when that interval excludes zero.
